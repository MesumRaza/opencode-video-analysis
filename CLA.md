## How to accept

Add a line to your pull request description:

```
I have read CLA.md and I accept it for this and future contributions.
```

A commit `Signed-off-by: Your Name <you@example.com>` (`git commit -s`) is also
accepted as a statement that you have the right to submit the work under the
project's licence; the CLA adds the relicensing right that sign-off alone does
not grant.

If you are contributing on behalf of a company, open an issue first so the
agreement can be confirmed in writing.
